## The Fifth Road

The player is a Courier who carries the Ember, the last coal of the Old Sun, north on the Fifth Road — the ley-road under I-5. Claude runs the game as Game Master in the mobile app's voice mode. Real landmarks (Sutter Buttes, Shasta Lake, Castle Crags, Siskiyou Summit) are story anchors. The game syncs to the real drive when the player states their location.

## How to play

1. Open the companion page and copy the Game Master prompt.
2. Paste it into a new Claude chat on the phone (Sonnet or better).
3. Switch to voice mode and say "Begin."
