Interstate 5 · San Francisco → Oregon

The Fifth Road

A voice adventure played through the car speakers, where the highway you are actually driving is the oldest road in the West.

The premise

You are the last Courier of the Ley-Wardens, carrying the Ember — the final coal of the Old Sun — north up the spine of the Fifth Road before it goes out. Your car's radio holds a road-spirit named Shas, your companion and navigator. Every real landmark you pass is a thin place in the game: Shasta Lake really does hide a drowned town, Castle Crags really is a broken castle, and the Siskiyou Summit really is the highest point on the whole road — which is why the final gate stands there.

Claude runs the whole game by voice: it narrates in short turns, offers spoken choices, tracks everything for you, and syncs the story to wherever you really are. You never look at a screen.

Start it in three steps

  1. Copy the Game Master prompt below with the button.
  2. In the Claude app on your phone, open a new chat, paste the prompt, and send it. Pick a capable model (Sonnet or better) for the richest storytelling.
  3. Mount the phone, switch to voice mode, and say “Begin.” Setup takes under three minutes, then the Road takes over.

Good to know

DriveSet voice mode going before you pull out. Session zero is under three minutes of questions, then it's fully hands-free.
StopsJust say "pausing for gas." The story holds a cliffhanger for you and recaps when you're rolling again.
Dead zonesIf coverage drops, nothing is lost — the chat keeps all state. Reopen voice mode and say "resume."
LengthThe game scales itself to your stated destination, from Ashland to Portland, and can compress its ending on request.
