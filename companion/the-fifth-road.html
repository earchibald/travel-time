Interstate 5 · San Francisco → Oregon

The Long Road North

Voice adventures played through the car speakers, where the highway you are actually driving is older and stranger than the map admits.

Choose your world

Three overlays on the same real drive: The Fifth Road (mythic wonder), The Dragon Road (crunchy fantasy RPG), and Corridor Five (sci-fi — the coast is a generation ship and I-5 is its axial corridor). All sync to wherever you really are, and all treat your electric car as what it plainly is in any honest mythology: a silent steed that drinks lightning. Charging stops are sacred — the games run their richest scenes while you're plugged in and parked.

The two prompt cards below are the original copy-paste versions, kept as a fallback. The zero-hands way to play all three worlds is the Console, one section down.

The Console — completely hands-free

One page runs everything: it listens through the mic, plays the Game Master through the speakers, watches the GPS itself, and holds all three worlds with their saves. Pausing, resuming, and jumping between worlds are voice commands. No copying, no pasting, no touching the phone after you tap Start.

  1. Open the Long Road Console in Safari on the phone, or Chrome on the laptop.
  2. In Settings, once: choose the connection — Claude bridge (laptop OAuth, run cd bridge && npm start), an Anthropic API key, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — and tap Test.
  3. Tap Start the drive, allow mic and location, and speak. Say pause, resume, switch to the dragon road, switch to corridor five — every world saves itself and picks up with a recap.

The Road Beacon — live GPS

A separate one-file page for mobile Safari that watches your real GPS, shows which thin place you are in, and speaks each arrival aloud — so you and the Game Master both hear "Approaching the Night Gate" without you tracking anything. It has both worlds' names built in, keeps the screen awake, and works offline once loaded.

It lives outside this page because claude.ai artifacts run in a sandbox with no location access. Open the hosted copy in Safari — it will ask once for location permission — or save the file as an offline backup.

Good to know

DriveSet voice mode going before you pull out. Setup is under three minutes of questions, then it's fully hands-free.
ChargingSay "charging at ___" and the game runs its richest parked scene, sized to your charge time — Lightning Wells in one world, Lightning Shrines in the other.
StopsJust say "pausing for gas." The story holds a cliffhanger and recaps when you're rolling again.
Dead zonesIf coverage drops, nothing is lost — the chat keeps all state. Reopen voice mode and say "resume."
LengthBoth games scale to your stated destination, Ashland to Portland, and compress their endings on request.

Kennett really is under Shasta Lake. Castle Crags really is a broken castle. The Siskiyou Summit really is the highest point on the whole road. Safe travels, Courier — or adventurer.
